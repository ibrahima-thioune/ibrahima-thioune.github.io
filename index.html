Connected beehive

3rd-year bachelor's degree in EEA, University of Toulouse, January to June 2026. Project carried out with two other students.

Goal: To design a connected beehive that remotely measures temperature, humidity and weight, and runs autonomously on solar energy.

Problem: The GPS module did not receive enough satellite signals to give a reliable position.

Solution: Due to lack of time, we collected position data with an external GPS, recorded on a phone.

Photovoltaic system for a medicine refrigerator in an isolated area

Final-year thesis, BTS in electrical engineering technology, CEDT Le G15, Senegal, 2023-2025.

Goal: To design a standalone photovoltaic system able to power a medical refrigerator reliably and continuously, to preserve the cold chain for medicines even without a power grid.

Problem: In isolated areas, access to electricity remains a major challenge and compromises the storage of temperature-sensitive medicines.

Solution: I sized the system from the refrigerator's consumption (0.35 kWh per day, about 500 Wh per day with 30% losses). With 5 peak sun hours in Senegal, at least 100 Wp are needed; I chose 150 Wp for margin. I then built a prototype with a solar panel on a metal frame, a charge controller, a battery and an inverter.
