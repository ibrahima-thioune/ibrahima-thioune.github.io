Study of a Connected Beehive – 3rd-Year Bachelor's Project (EEA)

University of Toulouse, January – June 2026. Team project (3 students).

We designed and validated a connected beehive prototype that remotely measures the hive's temperature, humidity and weight. The system is based on an Arduino Uno board, two temperature and humidity sensors (DHT11 outside, SHT31 inside), an HX711 weighing module, and Bluetooth data transmission to a phone.

We checked the communication protocols (One Wire, I2C, UART) using a PicoScope oscilloscope. A GPS was used to collect position data. Finally, we sized the solar power supply: a 5 W panel and a 20 Ah lithium-ion battery, for a theoretical autonomy of 5 days.

This project taught me how to program sensors, analyze signals, and size an energy-autonomous system.
